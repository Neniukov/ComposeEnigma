A creative playground for exploring animations, motion, and visual effects in Jetpack Compose.

- **Paper Worlds** — a clothbound book unfolds into a miniature alpine paper landscape. Drag to open and fold it away. [Interaction and implementation](docs/paper-worlds.md).
- **Hello, Android** — a procedural 3D Android robot that floats, waves and flips. Drag to turn it. [Interaction and implementation](docs/hello-android.md).
- **Kinetic Vinyl** — a tactile turntable with a paper sleeve, inertial rotation, and a moving tonearm. [Interaction and implementation](docs/kinetic-vinyl.md).
- **Liquid Portal** — a refracting window between two landscapes. [Interaction and implementation](docs/liquid-portal.md).
- **Journey** — a travel card that unfolds into a mountain escape. [Interaction and implementation](docs/journey.md).
